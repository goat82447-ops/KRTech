C#/.NET from zero to interview-pro

A 365-day guided path from programming foundations to building, operating, and explaining production-grade .NET systems. It covers the missing senior signals too: project deep dives, incident ownership, technical leadership, system design, and realistic mock interviews. This is a practice curriculum—not a promise of interview success.

Choose depth over rushing: If you already know a topic, complete its build, failure test, and explanation anyway. Record evidence from your own projects; never present invented experience as real.

Your daily 2–4 hour routine

The plan is designed around retrieval, implementation, and explanation—not passive video watching.

2 hours · minimum

30 min learn and diagram the concept; 70 min code the day's lab; 20 min closed-book recall and notes.

3 hours · recommended

Add 30 min writing tests, reproducing a failure, or debugging the lab; add 30 min explaining the choice aloud.

4 hours · stretch

Add 45 min integrating the feature into your capstone and 15 min updating your engineering log or interview story.

Keep one rest/flex day if life requires it: shift the plan, do not double the next day's workload. Every seventh day is deliberately a review/demo checkpoint.

Six stages: beginner to professional readiness

WEEKS 1–8 · DAYS 1–56

1 · Computing and programming foundations

CLI, Git, debugging, C# syntax, control flow, methods, problem solving, OOP and basic data structures.

WEEKS 9–18 · DAYS 57–126

2 · C# and computer science depth

Generics, collections, LINQ, delegates, async, concurrency, runtime, testing, SQL, indexes, transactions and modeling.

WEEKS 19–30 · DAYS 127–210

3 · Build professional .NET services

HTTP, ASP.NET Core, DI, APIs, security, EF Core, migrations, integrations, caching, background work and modular design.

WEEKS 31–39 · DAYS 211–273

4 · Distributed systems and operations

Messaging, consistency, system design, Azure, containers, delivery, observability, incident response and application security.

WEEKS 40–46 · DAYS 274–322

5 · Interview and portfolio engineering

DSA patterns, code quality, low-level design, architecture drills and a production-shaped portfolio capstone.

WEEKS 47–52 + DAY 365

6 · Senior ownership and interview readiness

Reliability, leadership, project narratives, system-design practice, mock loops, gap repair and a measurable final review.
